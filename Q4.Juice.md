# 4. Juice

The description points to a location near a juice shop. The flag format, `labyrinth{w1_w2_w3}`, shows the answer is three words. This matches what3words, a service that maps every small area in the world to a unique three-word address.

## Finding the location

Searching Google Maps for juice shops near MIT Manipal gives several results. The top five all look like plausible matches, so there's no way to tell which one is correct just by looking.

## Getting the three words

Taking each of the five shops' locations into what3words gives five different three-word addresses. Each one is formatted into the flag shape and submitted:

```text
labyrinth{w1_w2_w3}
```

Trying each in turn, the one that works is Fresh Juice World, giving:

```text
dabbling.arranges.reckon
```

## Flag

Replacing the dots with underscores to match the flag format:

```text
labyrinth{dabbling_arranges_reckon}
```
