# Eisodos

The challenge points to the Labyrinth CTF 2026 Discord server. The flag is split into two halves and hidden in the server's rule channels.

## First half

After joining the server, open the `#rules` channel. The first half of the flag is in the channel description:

```text
labyrinth{w3lc0m3_t0
```

## Second half

The `#ai-rules` channel hides the second half in the same way, in its channel description:

```text
_th3_labyrinth}
```

## Flag

Joining both halves gives:

```text
labyrinth{w3lc0m3_t0_th3_labyrinth}
```
